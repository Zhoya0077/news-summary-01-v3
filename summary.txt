World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: Russia and Ukraine ended two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi with a large prisoner-exchange deal but no ceasefire, Reuters reported; US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff head to Moscow on Thursday. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, hotter than expected, pushing the 10-year Treasury yield briefly above 5% and Wall Street lower; markets now price a December rate rise as near-certain (AP). Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases, with 5,387 deaths, the government said, the second-largest and second-deadliest outbreak on record (AP). Congolese President Felix Tshisekedi signed a law allowing constitutional change by referendum, which critics say could let him seek a third term (Reuters). Nvidia's board hired a former federal prosecutor to investigate chief executive Jensen Huang's ties to an alleged Chinese spy, after President Trump said the FBI was looking into it; Nvidia shares fell 5.5% (AP). Japan is expected to say it will consider nuclear-powered submarines as it rewrites its security documents, Asahi Shimbun reported. At least 65 people were killed in clashes in western Ethiopia near the Sudanese border (France 24).
